# Accessibility checklist

Calmpass should remain understandable and usable without relying on colour, fine pointer control, or animation.

- Use semantic landmarks and a logical heading order.
- Provide descriptive labels for interactive rooms, time controls, preference inputs, and actions.
- Support keyboard operation for every interactive control.
- Make focus indicators clearly visible.
- Aim for touch targets of at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels.
- Pair sensory colours with text, numbers, patterns, icons, or shape differences.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Keep contrast and line length comfortable.
- Test at a narrow mobile viewport, including 375px width.
- Disclose that sample conditions are illustrative and are not live measurements or predictions.
- Do not use diagnostic language or promise a place will be quiet, comfortable, or safe.
